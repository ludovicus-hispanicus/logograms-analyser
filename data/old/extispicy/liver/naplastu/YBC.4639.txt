@obverse
@column 1

1. šum-ma i-na a-mu-tim 4 na-ap-la-sa₃-tum!(TIM)
#tr.en: If on the liver four Views are present at the same time: the enemy will launch an attack against the heart of your city and will capture your cities and carry (their people) away.
2. iš-te-ni-iš iz-za-az-za
3. na-ak-rum a-na li-ib-bi a-li-i-ka
4. i-te-bi-a-am-ma a-la-ni-i-ka
5. i-ki-im-ma i-ta-ba-al

@column 2

6. šum-ma 4 na-ap-la-sa₃-tum qa₂-ab-lum
#tr.en: If there are four Views: battle will happen in the land.
7. i-na li-ib-bi ma-tim ib-[ba-aš-ši]
8. šum-ma [ina a-mu-tim] 4 na-ap-la-sa₃-tum
#tr.en: If [on the liver] there are four Views: usurper kings will rise up in the four world-regions; this one will fall, that one will rise.
9. šar-ru-u₂ ha-am-me-e ki-ib-ra-at
10. ma-a-tim i-te-bu-u₂-nim
11. an-nu-u₂-um i-ma-qu₂-ut an-nu-u₂-um i-te-be₂
12. šum-ma na-ap-la-aš-tum ki-ma un-qi₂-im
#tr.en: If the View is like a ring seal: the country will become enclosed and its mouth will revert to one (opening).
13. ma-a-tum u₂-te-es-si₂-ir
14. pi-i-ša a-na iš-te-en i-ta-ar
