object: Arnaud notes this is the top-left corner of a tablet, and that only about two signs appear to be missing at the edge.
arnaud_notes: "p. 317 — l. 1 MA, if it is that sign at all, is ill-formed; ll. 3, 6, 8, 9 and 15' are restored from l. 12'."
source: hand-transcribed from Arnaud's edition. NB Arnaud prints Sumerograms in lowercase roman and Akkadian syllabic in lowercase italic (French convention); Sumerograms are converted to UPPERCASE here per project convention. Arnaud's superscript collation ticks are rendered as "!". Adad ({d}U in the edition, l. 14) is normalized to {d}IŠKUR per corpus house style. Not in eBL (Msk excavation numbers return 404; the fragmentarium holds no Emar material).
note: Logograms uppercase, syllabic lowercase, determinatives in {}. Brackets = restoration; x = illegible (ignored). Recurring formula KI.MIN GAR ina N MU.MEŠ = (ditto) occurs within N years. Line 13' is a runover of 12', marked ":" by Arnaud and ($___$) here.
corrections: "Against the previous version — l. 1 DIB.DIB-su (not -šu); l. 2 restores the dropped A of A ÍD ('the water of the river', per Arnaud's French); l. 5 NU GÌR.BAL is uppercased, since Arnaud sets it roman i.e. as a Sumerogram, where the previous version had it lowercase as ambiguous (this raises the file's LDI); l. 7 su sa (not -šu ša); l. 13' LÚ šá (not lu ša)."
caveat: Poorly preserved. Its LDI rests largely on the repeated logographic prognostic formula KI.MIN GAR ina N MU.MEŠ and should NOT be read as evidence that this text is more logographic than the better-preserved CBS 12580.
