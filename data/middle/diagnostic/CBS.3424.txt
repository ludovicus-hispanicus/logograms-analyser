note: MB Nippur diagnostic text written, in Heeßel's words, "wie in altbabylonischer Zeit üblich, rein syllabisch geschrieben" — i.e. OB-style syllabic. The syllabic POLE of Middle-period variation, opposite to the logographic CBS 12580 from the same period/city. Transliteration after Heeßel; subscripts normalized; death apodosis BA.UŠ₂ = imât. Logograms uppercase, syllabic lowercase, {} det., [] restoration, x illegible. Not in eBL. INCLUDED in counts (legitimate MB witness; documents internal variability), not excluded like CBS 3831.
