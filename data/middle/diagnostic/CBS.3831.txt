note: >
  EXCLUDED FROM THE LDI. CBS 3831 is a genuine Middle Babylonian diagnostic
  extract from Nippur (the companion of CBS 12580, same Rutz 2011 study), but it
  is too fragmentary to score: Rutz states "a connected translation is impossible"
  and that only "certain fragmentary words and phrases on the obverse secure the
  tablet's identification." Of the few legible tokens, most are broken (x) or the
  non-lexical numeric tally "ilimmu.u". Its surviving content is NOT a divergent
  tradition — it parallels canonical Sakikkû 3 (qāt eṭemmi šaggāši "hand of the
  ghost of a murderer", obv. 4; ana muršišu itūr imât "he relapsed, he will die",
  obv. 5). It is held out of all counts (exclude: true) but its text lines are
  loadable, so it can be scored individually; the two numeric-tally lines
  (ilimmu.u) are commented out as non-omen text.
