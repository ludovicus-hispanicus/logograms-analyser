findspot: Scribal Quarter (Tablet Hill), area TA III/2, Locus 70 — a Neo-Babylonian stratum (632–538 BCE, per the dated-tablet list in OIP 78, 74–76), per the eBL team
counting: DIŠ
topic: prognosis by days of illness; head/temples (SAG.KI), eyes (IGI), kidneys (ÉLLAG), temple-veins (SA SAG.KI)
series: 2nd tablet of a pre-canonical Nippur series ana marṣi ina teḫêka (colophon, 169 lines); reordered relative to the canonical handbook (Labat 1956)
canonical_parallel: Sakikkû Tablets 3, 4, 5, 12, 16, 17, 18, 21, 22 (Schmidtchen 2021 concordance, book p.709-712 — Rs.2=3:108, Rs.4-6=3:107/105/30, Rs.7-8=5:64/71, Rs.10-14=4:89-101, Vs.1-5=16:65-74, Vs.10-12=18:10-12, Vs.15=17:41-42, Vs.18-19=17:69/78, Vs.6/23-24=21-22). NB at 16:45 (= obv. line 5 here) Labat/Heeßel read UD.5.KÁM but Labat's copy notes UD.6.KAM (Schmidtchen p.711 fn.4)
edition: Labat 1956, Syria 33, 119-130; tablet 2 NB 336 (= 2 N-T 336 = IM 57947), Nippur
dating: late Kassite / late Middle Babylonian by PALEOGRAPHY (Labat 1956:128 — ša not šá; ZAG/KAB not 15/2.30 for right/left; ṣu~ṣú; the šú forms preclude an early MB date, so it sits at the late end of MB). Its ARCHAEOLOGICAL findspot, however, is a Neo-Babylonian stratum (see findspot), which is why eBL dates IM 57947 'Neo-Babylonian'; the eBL team confirms the script nonetheless looks Kassite. Most likely a Kassite (late MB) composition preserved/recovered in a later context. Counted here as Middle Babylonian (date of composition).
note: >
  Sign-level reading collated by L. Sáenz against Labat's hand copy and photo
  (Syria 33, pl. V-VII); Labat's 1956 transliteration is normalized (logograms
  read as Akkadian), so it could not be scored directly. Right/left = ZAG/GUB₃
  on the tablet; SAG.KI=nakkaptu (temple), SA=šer'ānu (vein), IGI=īnū (eyes),
  SAG.DU=qaqqadu (head), ÉLLAG=kalītu (kidney), UMUŠ=ṭēmu. KI of SAG.KI written
  with a period (part of the logogram), not as a {} determinative. Residual
  uncertain signs '?'; damaged '#'; [] restoration; x illegible. Heavily
  fragmentary (line-ends broken throughout); apodoses largely lost.
  HELD OUT of the Middle aggregate (exclude: true) and used as a late-MB CASE
  STUDY in §III: at 0.67 (excl. particle) it sits near the Neo ceiling, and
  including it would raise the Middle diagnostic LDI from 0.49 to 0.57. Its
  high logographic density independently corroborates Labat's late-Kassite
  dating. Standalone score is informational only.
