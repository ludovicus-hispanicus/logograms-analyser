genre: diagnostic omens
period: Middle Assyrian
provenance: Assur
counting: DIŠ
topic: prognosis by Hand-of-the-god diagnoses (Sin dEN.ZU, Šamaš dUTU aššu kasap asirti = on account of silver of the sanctuary); head, temples (SAG.KI), eyes, penis
series: pre-canonical Middle Assyrian diagnostic text; a (partial) duplicate of VAT 10235 — entries 1-7 overlap VAT 10235 i 17'-23', then continue with Hand-of-Šamaš diagnoses (Heeßel 2010:178)
canonical_parallel: individual entries parallel canonical Sakikkû 12:5, 10:69, 11:19, 14:109 (Heeßel 2010:181 philological commentary)
edition: Heeßel 2010, Assur-Forschungen (Maul and Heeßel eds.) 178-181; tablet VAT 10748 (previously unpublished, identified by F. Köcher), Assur
dating: Middle Assyrian by paleography and syllabary (Heeßel 2010:175); pre-dates Esagil-kīn-apli's serialization. Counted here as Middle Assyrian.
note: >
  Transliteration after Heeßel's sign-level Umschrift (2010:178-179),
  directly scoreable (logograms preserved). One side only; cannot tell obverse
  from reverse. Lines 1-7 duplicate VAT 10235 i 17'-23'. mu-sar-ri-šu = his
  penis (bišru). Logograms uppercase, syllabic lowercase, {} determinative,
  [] restoration, x illegible. Heavily fragmentary.
