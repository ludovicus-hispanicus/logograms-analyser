---
genre: diagnostic omens
period: Middle Assyrian
provenance: Assur
counting: DIŠ
topic: prognosis with the single diagnosis Hand of Ištar (ŠU {d}INNIN) throughout; hair, nostrils, ears (GEŠTUG.II), gall (ZÉ), arm, hips (MURUB₄)
series: pre-canonical Middle Assyrian diagnostic text; ordered by a single identical DIAGNOSIS (Hand of Ištar) — same diagnosis-grouping principle as VAT 10235 and the MB Nippur texts (Heeßel 2010a:169)
canonical_parallel: individual entries parallel canonical Sakikkû 3:28, 6:60, 13:153, 8:13, 18:20, 10:67, 12:107/109 (Heeßel 2010a:184 philological commentary; cf. Schmidtchen 2021 — VAT 11122 Vs.1=3:25, Vs.2=4:38/39, Vs.6=4:39, Vs.7=19/20:9, Vs.10=4:89, Vs.11=11:84)
edition: Heeßel 2010a, Assur-Forschungen (Maul and Heeßel eds.) 180-183; tablet VAT 11122 (previously unpublished, identified by S. M. Maul), Assur
dating: Middle Assyrian by paleography and syllabary (Heeßel 2010a:175); pre-dates Esagil-kīn-apli's serialization. Counted here as Middle Assyrian.
note: >
  Transliteration after Heeßel's sign-level Umschrift (2010a:180-181),
  directly scoreable. Obverse only; reverse too damaged. {d}INNIN = Ištar
  written logographically. GEŠTUG.II = ears (uznā); ZÉ = gall (martu).
  Logograms uppercase, syllabic lowercase, {} determinative, [] restoration,
  x illegible. Fragment of the upper-left corner; fragmentary.
---
