coverage: COMPLETE. All 26 transliterated lines are given, using Arnaud's line numbers — ll. 1'-24' on p. 315, continuing with 25'-26' at the head of p. 316. Every line is primed because the top of the tablet is broken away; the previous version numbered them 1, 3-24 unprimed, omitted l. 2' (a bare scrap), and stopped at the p. 315 page break, missing 25'-26'. Arnaud gives no obverse/reverse division; none is asserted here.
arnaud_notes: "p. 317 — ll. 6'-7' allow the missing left portion to be gauged; 6' has a parallel at Labat TDP tablet 10 l. 27; 8' is not separated from 7' by a ruling and may be its apodosis; 11' LA is corrected from TE (scribal hesitation between te/lētu and lahû); 13' PI is very doubtful; 14' KÚ is written KAxME(?) not KAxNINDA; 16' KÚ is worse still; 20' the date indications at ll. 9'-10' are written with KÁM, so u₄].4.kam cannot be read."
source: hand-transcribed from Arnaud's edition. NB Arnaud prints Sumerograms in lowercase roman and Akkadian syllabic in lowercase italic (French convention); Sumerograms are converted to UPPERCASE here per project convention. Heavily fragmentary (both edges broken on most lines); genuinely ambiguous tokens left lowercase, conservatively, as at Emar 669:43. Arnaud's superscript collation ticks are rendered as "!". His díb (l. 15') is normalized to DIB per corpus house style; his maš.silà (l. 4') is kept as printed. Not in eBL (Msk excavation numbers return 404; the fragmentarium holds no Emar material).
note: Logograms uppercase, syllabic lowercase, determinatives in {}. Brackets = restoration; x = illegible (ignored). '?' marks uncertain reading. counting is line, not BE — only ll. 6', 7', 9', 10' and 11' open with BE, the rest having lost their opener to the break.
