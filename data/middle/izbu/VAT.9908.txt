counting: line
topic: šumma izbu aḫû — animal-birth ("extraneous") omens, animal section
publication: VAT 9908 = KAL 1 no. 47 (Heeßel 2007, Keilschrifttexte aus Assur literarischen Inhalts 1), Kopie S. 186-187
canonical_parallel: K 3669 (CT 27/50) Rs. 3'-12', K 3966 (CT 28/3-4) Vs. 20 - Rs. 11, K 1750 (unpubl.) Rs. 2'-10'
source_note: >
  Middle Assyrian tablet carrying terrestrial omina (obverse, too fragmentary to
  score — omitted here) and 'šumma izbu aḫû' animal omens (reverse, scored). Read
  off Heeßel's KAL 1 edition (no. 47). Half-brackets (damaged-but-legible) rendered
  as '#'; [ ] = restoration/break; x = illegible; {} = determinative. Notably
  logographic: Gilgameš = {d}BÌL.GA.MES, GÉME = amūtu "omen" (K 8639 parallel).
note: >
  The ONLY Middle Assyrian izbu witness presently known, and badly preserved
  (obverse lost, reverse a broken list of animal omens: goat, deer, horse, donkey,
  ox, lion, pig, turtle, wild-cow, raven, zebu, frog, fish, snake, bear). Scored for
  documentation; a single fragmentary tablet does not support a robust Middle-period
  Assyrian-Babylonian comparison.
