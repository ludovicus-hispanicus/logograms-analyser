note: "padanu (the Path, GIR2) section observing the LEFT path turning the gall-bladder (BE GIR2 GUB3 ZE2 BAL-ma...). Middle Babylonian, regular excavation at Babylon — a genuinely southern-Babylonian MB padanu witness (vs VAT 10788, MB-script found at Assur). A Path x gall-bladder hybrid: eBL tentatively classifies it under barutu ch. 6 (Martu), but every protasis leads with GIR2, so it is filed here under padanu. GIR2=path, GIR3=foot, ZE2=gall-bladder, GU.ZA=throne-base."
