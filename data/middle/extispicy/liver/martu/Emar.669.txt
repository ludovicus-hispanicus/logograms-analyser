coverage: COMPLETE. All 73 transliterated lines of Arnaud pp. 284-285 are given, using HIS line numbers. Analyses that want omens only should drop 71-73.
colophon: 62 martu omens (1 šūši 2 zé mu.bi.im = "60 + 2 gall-bladder (omens), that is their number"), invoking Nergal, Ea and Adad. Given as ll. 71-73. Arnaud's note ad l. 72 flags that the colophon's 62 fits the tablet badly — he counts 64 entries opening with BE.
uncertain_readings: Arnaud's note ad l. 58 warns that naglabu, "if that is the word to read", does not belong to the hepatoscopic vocabulary; the reading is his conjecture.
structure: Arnaud gives NO obverse/reverse division for this tablet; none is asserted here. Lines 38, 40, 42, 44, 55 and 57 are runovers (indented continuations) of the preceding line, marked ($___$) per corpus convention, and are counted as separate lines by Arnaud.
note: Hand-transcribed from Arnaud. He prints Sumerograms in lowercase roman and Akkadian syllabic in lowercase italic (French convention); Sumerograms are UPPERCASED here per project convention, and the syllabically written divine name iš-ha-ra (l. 52) is kept lowercase. Unidentified signs that Arnaud lists raw (l. 43 "bu sal za ba ra ka at", l. 48 "ši mu ta") are left lowercase, conservatively, since it is not established that they are logographic. GAG TI = sikkat ṣēli (peg of the rib). Logograms uppercase, syllabic lowercase, {} determinatives, [] restoration, <> scribal omission, x illegible. Not in eBL.
