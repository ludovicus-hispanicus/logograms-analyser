@obverse

1. BE [...] x tu SU.GU₇ IGI-[mar]
#tr.en: ... will experience a famine.
2. BE [URU ZAG] KUR₂ [NIGIN-ka]
#tr.en: ...: the enemy's [border-city will muster against you].
3. BE [URU ZA]G-ka ana KUR₂ N[IGIN]-ur
#tr.en: ...: your [bor]der-city will muster against the enemy.
4. BE [KUR₂] KUR N[UN] TI-qe₂
#tr.en: ...: [the enemy] will take the land of the prin[ce].
5. BE [ana {giš}]TUKUL SUḪ₃-u₂ ina U₄ SU₃ GU₃-mu x
#tr.en: ...: for battle, confusion; alternatively (in the distant future): wailing ...
6. BE [NU]N ṭa-ri-du-ta₅ DU-a[k]
#tr.en: ...: the prin[ce] will become an exile.
7. BE [URU] ZAG-ka KUR₂ DAB-bat
#tr.en: ...: your border-[city] the enemy will seize.
8. BE [URU ZA]G KUR KUR₂ DAB-bat
#tr.en: ...: the border-[city] of the enemy land you will seize.
9. BE [LUGAL KUR-su u]n-na-aš₂ I₃.[DUB SA₅] SU₃
#tr.en: ...: [the king] will weaken [his land], a [full] granary will become empty.
10. BE [KUR₂] ina MURUB₄ KUR-k[a ID₂] i-ḫe-er-ri
#tr.en: ...: [the enemy] will dig [a canal] in the midst of [your] land.

@reverse

11. BE [AD.ḪAL-k]a ana KUR₂ E₃-[ṣi]
#tr.en: ...: [your secret] will go out to the enemy.
