note: Hand-transcribed from Arnaud. He prints Sumerograms in lowercase roman and Akkadian syllabic in lowercase italic (French convention); Sumerograms are UPPERCASED here per project convention, and the syllabically written divine name iš₈-tár (ll. 59-60) is kept lowercase. The moon's bare numeral 30 is normalized to {d}30 (= Sîn, logographic) as elsewhere in the corpus — an editorial addition of the determinative only, with no effect on the count since determinatives are excluded. Arnaud's superscript collation ticks are rendered as "!". His tu₁₅.luₓ.lu is given as TU₁₅.U₁₈.LU (south wind) per corpus house style; his an.bir₈ (l. 19) is kept as printed rather than the corpus's AN.BIR₉. ba.ug₆ normalized to BA.UŠ₂. Line 26 is an indented separate line in the edition (not a typographic overflow) and is counted by Arnaud, so it is kept as its own line, marked ($___$); the flush-right overflows at ll. 22, 23, 31, 49 are NOT separate lines and are folded into their parent. counting is BE rather than line because every one of Arnaud's 66 lines opens with BE (preserved or restored) EXCEPT the two ($___$) runovers 26 and 66 — which the loader folds into their parent omen automatically, since the delimiter is tested at line start after bracket-stripping. This yields 64 omens over 66 lines, with an LDI identical to counting=line. Logograms uppercase, syllabic lowercase, {} determinative, [] restoration, <> scribal omission, x illegible. Not in eBL (Msk excavation numbers return 404; the fragmentarium holds no Emar material).
caveat: Lines 1-7 are the bare repeated opener [BE] 30 ina [na-an-mu-ri-šu with the apodosis wholly lost, and 63-66 are scraps. Restored [] material is counted in the default mode, so these lines contribute the formulaic protasis skeleton without their (potentially syllabic) apodoses; treat per-line figures for 1-7 and 60-66 as preservation artefacts.
