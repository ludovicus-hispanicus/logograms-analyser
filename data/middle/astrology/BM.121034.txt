---
genre: astrological omens
period: Middle Assyrian
provenance: Nineveh (Nabû Temple; Middle Assyrian tablet, probably imported from Assur)
counting: DIŠ
topic: lunar eclipse
series: Enūma Anu Enlil (transitional Assyrian recension; = Rochberg Text E, class IA)
reconstructed: true
edition: Rochberg-Halton, AfO Beih. 22 (1988), Appendix 1, pp. 273–279 (= EAE Text E)
note: >
  Tablet broken down the middle; in Rochberg's edition the PROTASES are largely
  restored from the Old Babylonian texts (A/B) and EAE 15, while what is actually
  preserved is mostly the APODOSIS (line ends). The text is "transitional": protasis
  written logographically, apodosis predominantly syllabic — the in-between data
  point for the diachronic logographic shift. Eclipse written AN.MI (the post-OB
  form; note AN.TA.LÚ retained only in rev. 24'). Restorations follow the editor's
  brackets. Logograms uppercase, syllabic lowercase, determinatives in {}.
