note: Hurrian cuneiform izbu omens. The "LDI" here = Sumerogram + AKKADOGRAM density (uppercase), NOT a logogram-vs-Akkadian-syllabic measure. Illustrative value bin LDI ≈ 0.871 (27/31) — ~4x the contemporary OB *Akkadian* izbu (≈0.13), because content words are written as Sumerograms (GEŠTU "ear", LUGAL "king", U₄ "day", LÚ.KÚR "enemy", NU GÁL "is missing") and Akkadograms (IZ-BU "izbu", -ŠU "his"), with only native grammar/verbs syllabic. Shows LDI tracks the graphic convention, not the spoken language. Tiny sample; not statistically robust. NB the Hurrian fragments are badly broken — only the logographic protasis-skeleton (DIŠ IZ-BU-ni-e GEŠTU… NU GÁL…) survives while syllabic apodoses are lost, so 0.871 is inflated by preservation (cf. the Emar 700 artifact); the Hittite ≈0.59 is the sounder figure.
