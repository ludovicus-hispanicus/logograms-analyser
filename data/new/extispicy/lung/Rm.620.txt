#tr.en: If the Middle Finger of the lung turns around in its place and its front parts face the Palace of the left lung — a land not belonging to the king will follow him; (same protasis:) on campaign the king and his courtiers will be in agreement; you will surround and seize an enemy fortress; the man's wife will strengthen his house; the gods of the enemy will side with the prince.
