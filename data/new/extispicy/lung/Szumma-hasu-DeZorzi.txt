@section Šumma ḫašû Tablet 3
15. BE KIR₄ ANŠE MUR ZAG GE₆ KUR₂ ana ur-pat-ia₅ IZI ŠUB-di
#tr.en: If the Tip of the Saw-horse of the right lung is black — the enemy will throw fire on my tents.
16. BE KIR₄ ANŠE MUR GUB₃ GE₆ ana ur-pat KUR₂ IZI ŠUB-di
#tr.en: If the Tip of the Saw-horse of the left lung is black — you will throw fire on the enemy's tents.
27. BE ANŠE MUR ša₂ ZAG EN GU₂.MUR ŠA₃-nu ZAG DU₈ URU ZAG-ia₅ KUR₂ DAB-ma u₂-še-ra-da
#tr.en: If the Saw-horse of the right lung is split inside up to the trachea on the right — the enemy will take my border town and carry off (booty downstream).
28. BE ANŠE MUR ša₂ GUB₃ EN GU₂.MUR ŠA₃-nu GUB₃ DU₈ URU ZAG KUR₂ DAB-ma u₂-še-ra-da
#tr.en: If the Saw-horse of the left lung is split inside up to the trachea on the left — I will take an enemy border town and carry off (booty downstream).
37. BE ANŠE MUR ša₂ ZAG SAG-su ina nap-šat ZAG KAR ina tak-ku-li-ia₅ KUR₂ TI-qe₂
#tr.en: If the Head of the Saw-horse of the right lung is atrophied at the right opening — the enemy will take my … .
38. BE ANŠE MUR ša₂ GUB₃ SAG-su ina nap-šat GUB₃ KAR ina tak-ku-li KUR₂ TI-qe₂
#tr.en: If the Head of the Saw-horse of the left lung is atrophied at the left opening — you will take … of the enemy.

@section Šumma ḫašû Tablet 13
29'. BE ŠU.SI MUR MURUB₄ ana ZAG GU₄.UD-iṭ u GAM.GAM-at URU ZAG KUR₂ NIGIN-ma ina KA DU₁₀.GA E₃-ka
#tr.en: If the Middle Finger of the lung jumps to the right and is pierced repeatedly — you will surround an enemy border city, but he will make you withdraw through blandishments.
70'. BE MIN MIN-ma IGI.MEŠ-ša₂ KUR MUR ša₂ ZAG IGI.MEŠ URU ZAG-ia₅ x [...] LUGAL u IDIM.MEŠ-šu₂ NU ŠE.GA.MEŠ DAM NA E₂-sa₃ [...]
#tr.en: If ditto (the Middle Finger) ditto (turns in its place) and its front parts face the Palace of the right lung — my border town … ; the king and his courtiers will not agree; the man's wife … her house … .
