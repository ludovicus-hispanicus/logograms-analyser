@obverse

1. šum₄-ma šu-me-el ZE₂ pa-ṭe₄-er um-ma-an {lu₂}KUR₂ ṣu₂-mi-ra-ti-ša u₂-ul i-ka-aš-ša-ad
#tr.en: If the left side of the gall-bladder is split: the enemy army will not reach its goals.
2. šum₄-ma i-na šu-me-el ZE₂ pi₂-iṭ-rum re-eš ZE₂ iṭ-ṭu₂-ul pu-um ki-nu-um
#tr.en: If on the left of the gall-bladder a fissure faces the top of the gall-bladder: a reliable word.
3. šum₄-ma i-na šu-me-el ZE₂ pi₂-iṭ-rum iš-di ŠU.SI iṭ-ṭu₂-ul ni-ip-ḫu pu-um la ki-nu-um
#tr.en: If on the left of the gall-bladder a fissure faces the base of the Finger: a niphu-mark, an unreliable utterance.
4. šum₄-ma i-na šu-me-el ZE₂ pi₂-iṭ-rum MAŠ₂ iṭ-ṭu₂-ul ni-ip-ḫa-at MAŠ₂.ŠU.GID₂.GID₂ RI.RI.GA um-ma-ni
#tr.en: If on the left of the gall-bladder a fissure faces the Increment: niphu-marks of the diviner, fall of the army.
5. šum₄-ma i-na šu-me-el ZE₂ pi₂-iṭ-rum ne₂-pe₂-el₂-ku ni-ip-ḫu
#tr.en: If on the left of the gall-bladder a fissure is spread out: a niphu-mark.
6. šum₄-ma i-na šu-me-el ZE₂ pi₂-iṭ-rum a-na GIR₃ iṣ-ḫi-ir a-na pi₂-iṭ-ri-im ir-bi ni-ip-ḫu
#tr.en: If on the left of the gall-bladder a fissure grows smaller toward the foot and larger toward the fissure: a niphu-mark.
7. šum₄-ma i-na šu-me-el ZE₂ pi₂-iṭ-rum i-na ŠA₃ pi₂-iṭ-ri URU{ki} ša a-na la-wi-šu ta-la-ku i-na-ad-di-ma it-ta-ṣi
#tr.en: If on the left of the gall-bladder a fissure lies within a fissure: the city you march to besiege will give up and march out;
8. ni-ip-ḫu pu-um la ki-nu-um mar-ṣu i-ma-at
#tr.en: a niphu-mark, an unreliable word, the sick man will die.
9. šum₄-ma i-na šu-me-el ZE₂ pi₂-iṭ-rum i-na ŠA₃ pi₂-iṭ-ri u₃ {giš}TUKUL im-ḫu-ur₂-šu
#tr.en: If on the left of the gall-bladder a fissure lies within a fissure and a mace faces it:
10. {lu₂}KUR₂ a-lak bi-ir-ti-šu i-na-ad-di-ma um-ma-an NUN i-ru-ub
#tr.en: the enemy will neglect the patrol of his fortress and the prince's army will enter it.
11. šum₄-ma i-na šu-me-el ZE₂ pi₂-iṭ-rum i-na ŠA₃ pi₂-iṭ-ri u₃ ši-lum na-di URU{ki} šu-ta-la-ku ta-ṣa-ba-at
#tr.en: If on the left of the gall-bladder a fissure lies within a fissure and a hole lies (in it): the city to which you march you will capture.
12. šum₄-ma i-na šu-me-el ZE₂ pi₂-iṭ-rum i-na ŠA₃ pi₂-iṭ-ri e-ri-iš-tum na-ad-di-a-at
#tr.en: If on the left of the gall-bladder a fissure lies and within the fissure a seed-grain lies:
13. NUN bi-ši qa₂-ti-šu u₂-ša-ad-da-nu-šu
#tr.en: they will force the prince to hand over his personal property.
14. šum₄-ma me-eḫ-re-et qu₂-tu-un ZE₂ pa-ṭe₄-er ar-bu-ut um-ma-ni
#tr.en: If the side opposite the narrow part of the gall-bladder is split: flight of the army.
15. šum₄-ma i-na šu-me-el ZE₂ pi₂-iṭ-rum 2 pi₂-šu-nu iš-te-en-ma um-ma-an {lu₂}KUR₂ ar-bu-tam i-la-ak
#tr.en: If on the left of the gall-bladder there are two fissures with a single opening: the enemy army will flee.
16. šum₄-ma šu-me-el ZE₂ a-di 2 pa-ṭe₄-er pu-um ki-nu-um
#tr.en: If the left side of the gall-bladder is split in two places: a reliable word.
17. šum₄-ma šu-me-el ZE₂ a-di 3 pa-ṭe₄-er ni-ip-ḫu
#tr.en: If the left side of the gall-bladder is split in three places: a niphu-mark.
18. šum₄-ma šu-me-el ZE₂ pi₂-iṭ-rum 4 5 6 a-di ma-du-ti pu-uṭ-ṭu₂-ur₂ gi-pi₂-iš um-ma-an {lu₂}KUR₂
#tr.en: If the left side of the gall-bladder is much split in four, five, six or many places: massing of the enemy army.
19. šum₄-ma i-na i-mi-ti ZE₂ pi₂-iṭ-rum MAŠ₂ iṭ-ṭu₂-ul šu-lum MU 1-KAM₂
#tr.en: If on the right of the gall-bladder a fissure faces the Increment: well-being for one year.
20. šum₄-ma ru-qu₂ ša i-mi-ti ZE₂ a-na i-di ZE₂ pa-ṭe₄-er ṣi-it ka-ab-tim
#tr.en: If the thin part on the right of the gall-bladder is split at the side of the gall-bladder: desertion of an important person;
21. a-na {giš}TUKUL i-si-il-ti um-ma-ni-ia ip-pa-ṭa-ar
#tr.en: for battle, control over my army will be lost.

@reverse

22. šum₄-ma me-eḫ-re-et re-eš ZE₂ pa-ṭe₄-er ṣi-it NUN ša-ni-iš mu-kilil re-eš le-mu-ut-tim
#tr.en: If the side opposite the top of the gall-bladder is split: flight of a prince; alternatively, a mukil-reš-lemutti demon,
23. qu₂-lum e-li um-ma-ni-ia i-ma-qu₂-ut
#tr.en: silence will fall upon my army.
24. šum₄-ma ni-si-iḫ ZE₂ i-mi-tam pa-ṭe₄-er um-ma-an-ka re-eš A.ŠA₃-ša u₂-ul i-ka-aš-ša-ad
#tr.en: If the nishu of the gall-bladder is split on the right: your army will not reach its destination.
25. šum₄-ma ni-si-iḫ ZE₂ šu-me-lam pa-ṭe₄-er um-ma-an {lu₂}KUR₂ re-eš A.ŠA₃-ša u₂-ul i-ka-aš-ša-ad
#tr.en: If the nishu of the gall-bladder is split on the left: the enemy army will not reach its destination.
26. šum₄-ma ni-si-iḫ ZE₂ i-mi-tam a-di 2 pa-ṭe₄-er NUN ti-il-la-as-su ip-pa-ṭa-ar-šu
#tr.en: If the nishu of the gall-bladder is split in two places on the right: the prince's auxiliaries will abandon him.
27. šum₄-ma ni-si-iḫ ZE₂ šu-me-lam a-di 2 pa-ṭe₄-er {lu₂}KUR₂ ti-il-la-as-su ip-pa-ṭa-ar-šu
#tr.en: If the nishu of the gall-bladder is split in two places on the left: the enemy's auxiliaries will abandon him.
28. šum₄-ma bi-ri-it i-mi-tim u₃ šu-me-lim i-na i-mi-tim ši-lum na-di NUN i-na pa-ṭi-šu i-ma-qu₂-ut
#tr.en: If between the right and left a hole lies on the right: the prince will fall at his border.
29. šum₄-ma bi-ri-it i-mi-tim u₃ šu-me-lim i-na šu-me-lim ši-lum na-di {lu₂}KUR₂ i-na pa-ṭi-šu i-ma-qu₂-ut
#tr.en: If between the right and left a hole lies on the left: the enemy will fall at his border.
30. šum₄-ma bi-ri-it i-mi-tim u₃ šu-me-lim i-na i-mi-tim ši-lum i-na ŠA₃ ši-lim na-di
#tr.en: If between the right and left on the right a hole lies within a hole:
31. um-ma-an NUN i-na na-ak-ba-ti-ša i-ma-qu₂-ut
#tr.en: the bulk of the prince's army will be defeated.
32. šum₄-ma bi-ri-it i-mi-tim u₃ šu-me-lim i-na šu-me-lim ši-lum i-na ŠA₃ ši-lim na-di
#tr.en: If between the right and left on the left a hole lies within a hole:
33. um-ma-an {lu₂}KUR₂ i-na na-ak-ba-ti-ša i-ma-qu₂-ut
#tr.en: the bulk of the enemy army will be defeated.
34. šum₄-ma i-na i-mi-ti ZE₂ ši-lum na-di-ma u₃ ra-ḫi-iṣ ki-lum um-ma-ni i-ṣa-ba-at
#tr.en: If on the right of the gall-bladder a hole lies and is destroyed: captivity will seize my army.
