@obverse

1. BE ZE₂ ka x mi i [...]
#tr.en: If the gall-bladder ... :
2. BE ZE₂ ina x ti KI.GUB [...]
#tr.en: If the gall-bladder ... the Presence ... :
3. BE ZE₂ UGU-nu KI.[GUB GAR-at ...]
#tr.en: If the gall-bladder lies above the Pre[sence: ...].
4. BE ZE₂ ina ZAG KI.GUB GAR-a[t ...]
#tr.en: If the gall-bladder lies to the right of the Presence: ...
5. BE ZE₂ ina GUB₃ KI.GUB GAR-a[t ...]
#tr.en: If the gall-bladder lies to the left of the Presence: ...
6. BE ina SAG ZE₂ GIŠ.ḪUR par-kat₂ [...]
#tr.en: If at the top of the gall-bladder a drawing lies crosswise: ...
7. BE ZE₂ ina MURUB₄-ša₂ i-pa saḫ-pat [{lu₂}ḪAL ana ik-ri-bi-šu₂ GUR-ar₂]
#tr.en: If the gall-bladder is covered with a membrane on its middle: [the diviner will return to his prayer].
8. BE SAG ZE₂ ana KI.TA-nu GU.MEŠ ka-m[i NUN LAL-mu]
#tr.en: If the tip of the gall-bladder is bound with threads on the underside: [the prince will be bound].
9. BE SIG ZE₂ GU DAB-ma A.MEŠ ZE₂ NU E₃.[MEŠ ERIN₂-ka BAD₄ DAB-bat]
#tr.en: If a thread holds the thin part of the gall-bladder and does not let the gall-bladder's fluid out: [your army will fall into distress].
10. BE ZE₂ kar-šu I₃ ŠA₃ ZAG u₃ GUB₃ [TAG.MEŠ ZI-bu ana NUN ip-pa-qid]
#tr.en: If the gall-bladder, the stomach and the fat-tissue right and left [are affected: attack; for the prince, (the extispicy) must be checked].
11. BE ZE₂ ka-la-ša₂-ma [TAG.MEŠ-at₂ AN.GE₆]
#tr.en: If the whole gall-bladder [is affected: eclipse].
12. BE ZE₂ ŠA₃-ša₂ ka-la-ša₂-ma [TAG.MEŠ AN.GE₆]
#tr.en: If the whole interior of the gall-bladder [is affected: eclipse].
13. BE SIG ZE₂ MAŠ.GAN₂ SILIM NIGIN-m[i KUR₂ ina KI.TUŠ-ia₅ GUB-az]
#tr.en: If the thin part of the gall-bladder surrounds the normal position of the Well-being: [the enemy will take my position].
14. BE SIG ZE₂ pi ši GUB₃ NIGI[N-mi ina KI.TUŠ KUR₂ GUB-az]
#tr.en: If the thin part of the gall-bladder surrounds the left ... : [you will take the enemy's position].
15. BE SAG ZE₂ IM SA₅ [nu-šur-re-e še-im]
#tr.en: If the tip of the gall-bladder is full of clay: [reduction of grain].

@reverse

16. BE MURUB₄ ZE₂ [IM SA₅ nu-šur-re-e E₂.SAG]
#tr.en: If the middle of the gall-bladder [is full of clay: reduction of the granary].
17. BE SIG ZE₂ IM SA₅ [nu-šur-re-e bu-lim]
#tr.en: If the thin part of the gall-bladder is full of clay: [reduction of livestock].
18. BE ZE₂ A.MEŠ-ša₂ ša₂-bu-lu-ma i+na x [...]
#tr.en: If the fluid of the gall-bladder is dried up and in ... :
19. BE ZE₂ A.MEŠ ṣe-em-ret-ma [ina ŠA₃-ša₂ DI-ḫu ŠUB-di ILLU u AN-u₂ IGI.IGI-lu₄]
#tr.en: If the gall-bladder is swollen with fluid and [a cyst lies in its midst: flood and rain will be equal].
20. BE ZE₂ TA ZAG ana GUB₃ zi-r[at KUR-ka ana KUR KUR₂ NIGIN-ur₂]
#tr.en: If the gall-bladder is twisted from right to left: [your land will muster against the enemy land].
21. BE ZE₂ TA GUB₃ ana ZAG zi-r[at KUR KUR₂ ana NUN NIGIN-ur₂]
#tr.en: If the gall-bladder is twisted from left to right: [the enemy land will muster against the prince].
22. BE ZE₂ ana ZAG TAB-e[t NUN ṭar-du ana URU-šu₂ GUR-ar]
#tr.en: If the gall-bladder is doubled on the right: [an exiled prince will return to his city].
23. BE ZE₂ ana GUB₃ TAB-e[t NUN ša₂ KUR KUR₂ ana URU-šu₂ GUR-ar]
#tr.en: If the gall-bladder is doubled on the left: [a prince of the enemy land will return to his city].
24. BE ZE₂ ana ZAG TAB-ma x [{giš}TUKUL IGI.IGI NUN ṭar-du ana URU-šu₂ GUR-ma GAZ.MEŠ-šu₂]
#tr.en: If the gall-bladder is doubled on the right and [points to a mace: an exiled prince will return to his city but will be killed].
25. BE ZE₂ ana GUB₃ TAB-ma x [{giš}TUKUL IGI.IGI NUN ša₂ KUR KUR₂ ana URU-šu₂ GUR-ma GAZ.MEŠ-šu₂]
#tr.en: If the gall-bladder is doubled on the left and [points to a mace: a prince of the enemy land will return to his city but will be killed].
26. BE ZE₂ lu-ḫa saḫ-pat [LUGAL ŠU KUR-ad₂]
#tr.en: If the gall-bladder is covered with a ... : [the king will reach his goal].
27. BE SAG ZE₂ ši-ši-tu 1 ŠU.SI u-[lu 2 ŠU.SI ar₂-mi LUGAL ŠU KUR-ad₂ ana MAŠ.EN.GAG kiš-pi DAB.MEŠ-šu₂]
#tr.en: If the tip of the gall-bladder is covered with a membrane one [or two fingers (wide): the king will reach his goal; for a poor man, witchcraft will seize him].
