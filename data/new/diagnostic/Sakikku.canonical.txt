topic: symptoms — cords (SA = šer'ānu) of hands/feet; head

edition: canonical first-millennium parallels to CBS 12580, quoted in Rutz 2011 (ZA 101), Appendix A; ultimately from TDP/Labat 1951 (Sakikkû 11 source A), BAM VII/Geller 2005 (Sakikkû 14 source C), and Goetze 1948 (Sakikkû 3, MLC 2639). The cross-milieu parallels Sakikkû 3:79 and 3:86-90 (second @-section) are added after Schmidtchen 2021 (BAM 13) 254-255, the composite of mss A (BM 33424) / C / D / I; Sakikkû 17:82 is added after Heeßel 2000 (AOAT 43) Tafel 17, composite of mss A20'/B15/E22.
source: hand-transcribed from Rutz 2011 Appendix A (the canonical duplicates under each CBS 12580 line) and, for the 3:79/3:86-90 block, from Schmidtchen 2021's edition (score read off the rendered pages). Lowercase Sumerograms converted to UPPERCASE per project convention.

  omens as the Middle Babylonian CBS 12580 (Nippur). The second block holds the

  3:86-90 pustule block (canonical TA…EN…KI.NÁ ≈ Assur VAT 10235:19-20, vs
  Ḫattuša syllabic iš-tu…a-di…ma-ia-li). Each canonical entry is tagged with its
  Sakikkû tablet:line and its parallel witnesses in a # comment above it.
